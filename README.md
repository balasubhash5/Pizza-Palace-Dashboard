# Pizza-Palace-Dashboard
Developed an interactive Pizza Sales Dashboard using Power BI to analyze pizza sales, quantities, pricing by category and size, top-selling pizza types, and monthly revenue trends. Used interactive visualizations to identify customer preferences and sales patterns for data-driven business decisions.
Project Overview

The Pizza Palace Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze pizza sales performance, product preferences, pricing, and monthly revenue trends. It helps understand which pizza categories, sizes, and types contribute most to overall sales.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze pizza sales quantity across different categories.

Compare average prices by pizza category and size.

Identify top-performing pizza types by total sales amount.

Understand sales distribution across pizza sizes.

Track monthly revenue trends and fluctuations.

Generate insights to support sales and business planning.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

DAX – Measures and calculations

Data Visualization – Pie charts, bar charts, stacked columns, and line charts

📊 Dashboard Features

1. Pizza Quantity by Category

Displays the quantity sold across Classic, Supreme, Veggie, and Chicken pizza categories.

2. Average Price by Category and Size

Compares average pizza prices across categories and sizes to understand pricing differences.

3. Sales by Pizza Type

Ranks pizza types by total sales amount to highlight top-performing products.

4. Quantity by Category and Size

Shows how different pizza sizes contribute to sales quantity within each category.

5. Sales by Pizza Size

Compares total sales amounts for L, M, S, XL, and XXL pizza sizes.

6. Monthly Sales Analysis

Visualizes monthly revenue trends to identify peak and low-performing months.

💡 Key Insights

Classic pizzas contribute the largest share of quantity sold among the displayed categories.

Large and medium pizzas account for substantial sales quantities.

Thai Chicken, BBQ Chicken, and California Chicken appear among the highest-revenue pizza types shown.

Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, with July showing a notable peak and September–October recording lower sales.

 Business Benefits
